This is the splash page for our group. The links above direct towards the other pages for this website.

We made this website to recognize our tech hero and to give resources to people trying to find more information about how to recognize biases within the tech space. The development of bias within the tech world continuously writes over the progress and achievements of people of color who have founded the world we feel comfortable working in. It is important to recognize every step of progress that has been made, and even more vital to recognize bias in our current environment and apply inclusive practices as we move forwards. We hope the pages above help you find your voice within the space as well.
