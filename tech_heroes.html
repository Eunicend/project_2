Our Tech Hero

Timnit Gebru

Who She is:

Timnit Gebru is an Ethiopian American computer scientist, founder and executive director of the Distributed Artificial Intelligence Research Institute (DAIR), and former co lead of the Ethical Artificial Intelligence team at Google.

How She Challenges Norms

She challenges the tech industry's rush to deploy larger AI systems without considering their hidden harms. Her research exposed how facial recognition software fails for dark skinned women, how AI training datasets can absorb human bias if not properly documented, and how massive language models can carry high carbon footprints while repeating harmful historical prejudices.

Why We Chose Her

Dr. Timnit Gebru stood by her research on the dangers of AI bias even when pressured by corporate leadership, standing her ground which led to her high profile departure from Google. Her willingness to risk her career with a tech giant such as Google rather than stay silent highlighted systemic corporate censorship and inspired broader legal and industry scrutiny surrounding discrimination and worker rights in Big Tech. She also went on to co found Black in AI, a nonprofit that works to increase the presence, inclusion, visibility and health of Black people in the field of AI.
